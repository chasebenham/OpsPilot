# Business case

Meridian Group is a fictional B2B professional services company of 200 employees across IT, Finance, Marketing, Operations, and People & HR. A planning assumption of approximately 600 internal requests per month illustrates the cost of fragmented intake, manual handoffs and inconsistent visibility; it is not measured production data.

The planned workflows are IT incidents, purchase approvals, and cross-department projects. A shared intake and audit trail can reduce manual triage and make ownership visible. Later approval and policy modules can support consistent purchasing rules, while coordinated project tasks can reduce missed handoffs.

Future measurement should establish a baseline and compare time to first response, time to resolution, handoff count, request aging, approval cycle time and requester satisfaction by type and department. Track data quality and fairness before attributing changes to automation; no benefit is claimed as measured in this prototype.
