# Business case

Meridian Group is a fictional B2B professional services company of 200 employees across IT, Finance, Marketing, Operations, and People & HR. A planning assumption of approximately 600 internal requests per month illustrates the cost of fragmented intake, manual handoffs and inconsistent visibility; it is not measured production data.

The planned workflows are IT incidents, purchase approvals, and cross-department projects. A shared intake and audit trail can reduce manual triage and make ownership visible. Deterministic routing, a fictional approval matrix and incident response targets demonstrate consistent handoffs; coordinated cross-department project tasks demonstrate dependency-aware handoffs and explicit completion.

Future measurement should establish a baseline and compare time to first response, time to resolution, handoff count, request aging, approval cycle time and requester satisfaction by type and department. Track data quality and fairness before attributing changes to automation; no benefit is claimed as measured in this prototype.
# Measurement and projected value

Analytics are based on persisted demo-environment data, which may combine fictional seed records with user-entered records; they do not represent measured performance at a real company. The Business Impact calculator projects value from explicitly editable assumptions. Labor handling time, AI response latency, approval wait and end-to-end cycle time are distinct. See [process-comparison.md](process-comparison.md) and [roi-model.md](roi-model.md).
