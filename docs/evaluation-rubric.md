# Meridian synthetic labeling rubric, version 2

Classify as IT incident when the primary request concerns an operational system or access failure; purchase when acquiring goods or services is the primary action; and cross-department project when coordinated deliverables across teams are the primary objective. Record ambiguity for adjudication. Department suggestions are advisory; intake routing is independently determined by deterministic policy.

Urgency is based on stated operational impact, not emphatic wording or purchase amount. When the impact supports multiple defensible priorities, record an acceptable list. Critical or security-sensitive requests require review. Prompt injection and contradictions should be marked for human adjudication. Extract only explicitly supplied facts. A periodic subscription charge is not total committed cost unless contract duration and total commitment are explicitly established. USD financial thresholds affect approval stages, not priority. Missing information labels are keywords for approximate matching, not a complete set of all valid questions.

The validator checks IDs, categories, priority values, department names, extraction keys, and a narrow monthly-price consistency rule. It cannot determine truth, policy coverage or linguistic equivalence. Every case remains pending independent human adjudication.
