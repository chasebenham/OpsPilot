# Purchase approval workflows

A purchase first goes to Finance intake. A structured purchase record stores the vendor, description, total committed amount as NUMERIC, USD currency, justification, contract term and the requester's department. The requester must explicitly confirm that the amount represents the full contract commitment; entering a monthly price does not establish the total. Missing fields, an unsupported currency or missing commitment confirmation hold approval generation. Send amounts as decimal strings; JSON floating-point values are rejected.

Stages are sequential: department manager first, then Finance for commitments of $1,000 or more; for $5,000–$19,999.99 add Operations Director, and for $20,000 or more add Executive Approver. Amounts under $1,000 require the department manager only. Finance approvers have the existing role; director and executive are additional capabilities, so existing roles remain valid. Stages are assigned to the lowest-ID active eligible user other than the requester; an unresolved stage blocks the cycle. An administrator or authorized manager can invalidate and regenerate a cycle with a reason after correcting missing approvers. Changing any stored purchase field invalidates the current stages; prior decisions, actors and audit events are retained. Rejected purchases need an explicit new submission cycle.

Only the assigned active eligible approver may decide the current stage, and the requester cannot approve their own request. Rejections require a reason. Requests are locked before stage updates; retrying the same decision and reason by the same actor is idempotent, while conflicting or out-of-order decisions are rejected. Financial authorization is derived from all stages in the latest cycle and is distinct from request fulfillment. Generic transitions into In Progress, Resolved or Closed are blocked unless the current purchase cycle is Authorized. No purchase, vendor payment, email or transfer is performed.

For a demonstration, select a demo employee, create a Purchase Request with complete USD details and the full-commitment confirmation, then switch to each listed assigned approver in sequence. Open Approvals and the linked request, review the policy and prior decisions, and explicitly confirm each decision. Edit a material detail to observe invalidation and start a new cycle. Changing user identity through the demo switcher is not authentication.
